How this works an automated data pipeline

1 · Collectn8n pulls headlines from Economic Times, Livemint, BusinessLine and RBI feeds.
2 · CleanOld news, filler posts and duplicate stories are filtered out.
3 · UnderstandGoogle Gemini summarises, categorises and scores each story's sentiment and importance.
4 · StoreEvery story is saved to Google Sheets, building a daily dataset.
5 · PublishSubscribers get an email, and this page is rebuilt via a GitHub commit.
Summaries are generated automatically by AI from public news feeds and may contain errors. Not investment advice. Headlines link to the original publishers. Built with n8n, Google Gemini, Google Sheets and GitHub Pages as an MBA Data Engineering & Web Hosting project.
