Privacy Policy

The Bombay Brief AI

Last updated: 13 November 2026

The Bombay Brief AI is a non-commercial student project built for an MBA course in Data Engineering & Web Hosting. It sends a daily, AI-summarised brief of public Indian market and finance news to people who choose to subscribe.

What we collect

  • Subscribers: your name and email address, provided when you subscribe, plus the date you subscribed.
  • News data: headlines and links from public RSS news feeds. This is not personal data.

How it is used

  • Your email address is used only to send you the daily brief.
  • Subscriber details are stored in a private Google Sheet accessible only to the project owner.
  • News headlines are sent to Google Gemini to generate summaries. Subscriber details are never sent to any AI service.

Google account access

The project's automation (n8n) connects to the project owner's own Google account to read and write the project Google Sheet and to send the brief via Gmail. It does not access any other user's Google account or data. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone.

Unsubscribing and deletion

Reply to any brief with "unsubscribe" and your details will be removed. When the project ends, all subscriber data will be deleted.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent by replying to any brief email.
